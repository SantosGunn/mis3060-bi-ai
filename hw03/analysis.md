# Corporate Events Timeline Analysis

Of the 33 executive-event rows, 23 were filed more than seven days before the nearest earnings filing, seven were filed more than seven days after, and three were within seven days. Apple, NVIDIA, and Walmart had more events classified as before earnings, and all six JPMorgan events fell into that category, while Microsoft had more after earnings. The three same-week events were all from Walmart's November 14, 2025 filing, six days before its earnings filing. Overall, the results lean toward changes being disclosed before earnings, but several rows represent different roles or people announced in the same filing, so they are not independent announcements. This comparison uses filing dates and only four earnings filings per company, so it does not establish that earnings caused the executive changes.
